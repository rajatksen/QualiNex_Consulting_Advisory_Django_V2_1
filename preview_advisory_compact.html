HOW AN ADVISORY ENGAGEMENT WORKS

Evidence first. Intervention second. Capability transfer throughout.

01

Frame the problem

Clarify the customer, operational, risk or management outcome that needs to change.

02

Build the fact base

Use data, process evidence, stakeholder input and observation to separate signal from symptom.

03

Change the system

Strengthen the process, controls, governance, problem-solving method or capability that drives the result.

04

Transfer and sustain

Embed ownership, management routines, measures and capability so the intervention can continue without dependency.
