WHY QUALINEX

Not a slide-deck consultancy. A practitioner brought in to change the operating system.

QualiNex is built around the difficult problems that appear between plant performance, customer expectations, supplier capability, risk and management governance. The proposition is deliberately practical: establish the fact base, identify the economic and quality drivers, intervene at the process level, and leave behind capability and management routines.

The practice draws on Subhamjit Deb’s senior manufacturing quality and operational-excellence career, global quality-system deployments, factory setups, Lean Six Sigma delivery, customer/supplier quality leadership and capability development.
