QUALINEX METHOD

Diagnose. Design. Deploy. Sustain.

A consulting method built for complex manufacturing problems where the answer must survive beyond the consultant.

Diagnose

Establish the fact base: customer voice, performance, process capability, failure modes, risk, governance and economic impact.

Design

Translate findings into a target operating model, prioritised value agenda, control architecture and implementation roadmap.

Deploy

Lead the critical interventions, coach teams, establish management routines and make ownership explicit.

Sustain

Embed standard work, capability, KPI reviews and governance so improvement survives the consulting period.

ADVISORY ASSURANCE

Every engagement should answer five questions.

What is the fact pattern?Evidence, data, customer voice and process observation.
What is the economic impact?COPQ, productivity, risk, launch cost or customer consequence.
What is the root cause?System, process, capability and behavioural causes — separated.
What changes now?Owners, milestones, controls and management routines.
How do we know it stayed changed?KPI trend, audit evidence, recurrence rate and capability adoption.
